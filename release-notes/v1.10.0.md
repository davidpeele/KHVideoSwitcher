## Audio meter

- New audio input level meter, spanning the full width of the PROGRAM pane, with a color-coded green/yellow/red scale, a dB ruler, and live numeric readouts per channel
- Audio input device picker moved into Settings (it's normally a set-once choice)
- New Settings controls: show/hide the meter, and a gain-calibration slider for line-ins that peg the meter during normal speech

## Toolbar

- The camera and screen buttons are now labeled **Capture Camera** and **Capture Screen**, and turn blue while active (previously "Start"/"Stop" and "Capture"/"Captured")
- The PREVIEW zoom slider now has a larger handle, sized and centered to match the audio meter next to it

## Over-the-shoulder

- New background color picker for the space revealed off-camera when the frame shifts for the picture-in-picture box (presets: black, off-white, beige, white, gray, or a custom RGB mix)
- New optional thin border around the picture-in-picture box, with its own color picker
- Fixed the OTS box preview in Settings, which didn't match where the box actually appears in PREVIEW

## Other

- Settings window is now scrollable and reorganized, so it can't run out of room as options are added
- Help updated to document every Settings control
