## New: keep the OTS inset off the speaker

Over-the-shoulder frames the camera fullscreen with the captured media boxed into the top-right corner, which can land right on a close-up speaker's face. A new **SHIFT CAMERA FOR OTS** setting (off by default, in Settings) pans the camera left by about 5% of the frame whenever OVER-THE-SHOULDER is showing — panning within the shot if you're digitally zoomed in, or sliding the whole frame left (revealing the black background on the right edge) if you're at full-frame/1x zoom.

## Layout refinements

- CAM/MEDIA/OTS are now three wide, evenly spaced tiles instead of one bordered strip.
- Wide/Take/Cut are equal-width buttons pinned to the right of the transport row.
- The toolbar now separates capture controls (camera/media pickers, Start, Capture) on the left from app-level actions (Virtual Camera, Settings, Compact) on the right.
- The zoom slider spans the full width of the PREVIEW pane.
