## New: Always Full Screen First

A new **Always Full Screen First** setting (off by default, in Settings) makes AUTO
SCENES send both videos and still pictures to full-screen MEDIA, instead of boxing
stills into the smaller over-the-shoulder corner. Turn it on if your hall prefers
slides and pictures to always fill the whole screen first — over-the-shoulder is
still available any time by switching to it manually (OTS / F3).

## New: in-app Help

A **Help** button in the toolbar opens a plain-English user guide in its own
window — it stays open alongside the app so you can browse it while you work.
It walks through every button and setting, keyboard shortcuts, how the app
works with JW Library and Zoom, and a handful of worked meeting scenarios for
new or non-technical operators.
