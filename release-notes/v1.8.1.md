## Fix: media capture going gray on still pictures

Capturing a monitor or window could turn gray after a second or two of no
motion — a still slide (like JW Library's year text) would show fine at
first, then blank out until something moved on screen. This was the app
mistaking "the picture hasn't changed" for "the feed died." Still
pictures now stay on screen correctly instead of going gray.

## Capture button now says "Captured"

The Capture button used to say "Capturing..." the whole time it was
running, which read as still-in-progress. It now says "Captured" once
capture has started.
