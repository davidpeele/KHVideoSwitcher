## New: Report a Bug

A "Report Bug" button in the toolbar lets testers put together a bug report
without hunting for logs themselves — app version, OS info, your camera and
capture device, and the recent activity log, all shown on screen for you to
review (and edit or delete anything) before you do anything with it. From
there you can copy it to your clipboard or open a prefilled GitHub issue —
nothing is ever sent automatically.

The app also now keeps a small local activity log
(`%APPDATA%\KHVideoSwitcher\logs`) to make troubleshooting easier, with your
Windows username automatically stripped from anything it writes.
